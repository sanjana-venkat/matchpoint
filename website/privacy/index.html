Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Matchpoint helps people discover sports partners, communicate, arrange matches, record results, and participate in local sports communities. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the Matchpoint iOS app.

Information we collect

Account and profile information

We collect information such as your email address, name, username, age or date of birth, gender selection, profile avatar, biography, sports, ratings, availability, and profile preferences.

Location information

With your permission, we collect device location to find nearby players, courts, clubs, and groups. Exact coordinates are kept private and used for discovery calculations. Other players receive only an approximate, neighborhood-level location. You can deny or revoke location permission in iOS Settings.

Social and match information

We collect connection requests, blocks, reports, direct messages, challenges, proposed times, venues, match participants, submitted scores, verification or dispute decisions, rating history, peer reviews, club memberships, and groups.

Device and service information

We may process device identifiers needed for account security and push-notification delivery. Our infrastructure providers may process network and technical information required to deliver and secure the service.

How we use information

  • Create and secure accounts.
  • Provide profiles, discovery, maps, chat, challenges, and match history.
  • Calculate and display sport-specific ratings.
  • Show nearby courts, clubs, and groups.
  • Deliver service messages and, with permission, push notifications.
  • Investigate reports, enforce community rules, prevent abuse, and protect users.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, and improve Matchpoint.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

How information is shared

Profile information, approximate location, sports, ratings, availability, match history, and community participation may be visible to other Matchpoint users as described in the app. Messages are shared with their intended participants. Reports may be reviewed by authorized safety personnel.

We use service providers to operate Matchpoint, including Supabase for authentication, database, realtime, and backend infrastructure, and Apple services for maps, device capabilities, and app distribution. These providers process information under their applicable terms.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transaction.

Retention and account deletion

We retain information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and rating integrity, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can request account deletion inside Matchpoint. Some information may be retained where required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or de-identified service records.

Your choices

You can update profile information and notification preferences in the app. You can disable location or notifications in iOS Settings. You can block or report another player and request account deletion inside the app.

Residents of some jurisdictions may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information. Contact us to exercise an applicable right.

Children

Matchpoint is not directed to children under 13, and the app requires users to state that they are at least 13. If we learn that an ineligible child provided personal information, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

Security and international processing

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, although no online service can guarantee absolute security. Matchpoint and its providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards apply to international transfers.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Matchpoint changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when legally required.

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to matchpoint.app.support@gmail.com.
