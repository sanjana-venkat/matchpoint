Help center

How can we help?

Email matchpoint.app.support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, Match Point version, and a short description of what happened. Never send your password or verification code.

Account and sign-in

Check spam or junk folders for confirmation and password-reset emails. Confirmation links can expire; request a new email from the sign-in screen. Match Point support will never ask for your password or Apple verification code.

Location

Location is optional. To change permission, open iOS Settings, find Match Point, select Location, and choose the access level you prefer. Other players see only an approximate neighborhood location.

Notifications

To change push permission, open iOS Settings, select Notifications, then Match Point. Notification categories can also be adjusted from Match Point’s profile settings.

Safety

Use Block and Report on a player profile if another user behaves inappropriately. For an immediate safety threat, contact local emergency services. Match Point is not an emergency service.

Delete your account

Open Profile, go to account configuration, and select Reset/Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps. For help, email us from the address associated with your account.

Beta feedback

Beta testers can report a problem through TestFlight or email support. Include the steps that led to the problem and a screenshot when it is safe to do so.
