# Match Point Support — draft

Need help with Match Point? Contact **matchpoint.app.support@gmail.com** and include your iPhone
model, iOS version, Match Point version, and a short description of what
happened. Do not email your password or verification code.

## Account and sign-in

- Check spam or junk folders for confirmation and password-reset email.
- Confirmation links can expire; request a new email from the sign-in screen.
- Match Point support will never ask for your password or Apple verification
  code.

## Location

Location is optional. To change permission, open iOS Settings, find Match Point,
select Location, and choose the access level you prefer. The map shows other
players only at an approximate neighborhood location.

## Notifications

To change push permission, open iOS Settings, select Notifications, then Match
Point. Notification categories can also be adjusted from Match Point’s profile
settings when push delivery is available.

## Safety

Use the Block and Report controls on a player profile if another user behaves
inappropriately. For an immediate safety threat, contact local emergency
services. Match Point is not an emergency service.

## Delete your account

Open Profile, choose account configuration, and select Reset/Delete Account.
Follow the confirmation steps. For help with deletion, contact
**matchpoint.app.support@gmail.com** from the email address associated with the
account.

## Beta feedback

Beta testers can report a problem through TestFlight or email
**matchpoint.app.support@gmail.com**. Include the steps that led to the problem and a screenshot when it is
safe to do so.
